# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

This project welcomes contributors of every background. To keep the community healthy, we ask everyone to:

- Be respectful, even in disagreement.
- Assume good faith.
- Critique code, not people.
- Refuse to use the project for stalking, harassment, doxxing, or any unauthorised investigation. Tools like this are easy to misuse; the community draws the line clearly.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Personal attacks, slurs, or harassment of any kind.
- Sharing private information about a third party without their consent.
- Encouraging or facilitating illegal use of the project.
- Sustained disruption of discussion.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, commits, code, issues, or contributors that violate this code. Serious or repeated violations may lead to a permanent ban.

To report a violation, open an issue tagged `code-of-conduct` or contact the maintainer privately.
